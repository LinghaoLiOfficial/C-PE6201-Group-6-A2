# Member case submission - English handoff

Use `../output/pdf/Group6_ProblemA_Case_Design_Request_EN.pdf` (v1.1) as the
primary member handout. Its editable text is `../docs/CASE_DESIGN_REQUEST_EN.md`.
The earlier Chinese version remains available as a reference.

Copy the three files in `templates/`, rename NAME to your own name, and submit
in `cases_NAME/`. Fill five new claims and five independently derived labels
(four ACT, one assigned negative), supporting rows and the design explanation.
The JSON templates are intentionally empty; they are not finished cases.

`reference_examples/` contains three instructor cases and their exact labels:
CLM-8842 (ACT), CLM-8894 (ASK), CLM-8925 (ESCALATE). Learn from their format and
reasoning, but do not append them to EXTRA_CLAIMS or count them as your own cases.
They are already in the shipped 15, which must remain in the final evaluation set.

The rule is explicit in `materials/A2_reference_data/PE6201_A2_Adding_Extra_Cases.pdf`:
p1 says never edit or delete a shipped row; p4 says the answer key retains the
shipped 15 plus the newly authored labels. The proposed final set is 15 + 30 = 45.

A ready-to-send ZIP is at `../output/handouts/Group6_Case_Design_Pack_EN.zip`.
No teammate cases have been merged by preparing these materials.
